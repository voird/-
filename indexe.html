Autobiography

Education and School Years

My interest in information technology and modern digital tools gradually took shape during my school years. I studied at Gymnasium No. 92 in the city of Krasnodar. During my studies, I strived not only to earn good grades but also to study the subjects that interested me in depth, to develop analytical thinking and independent work skills.

I graduated from the gymnasium with honours, receiving a certificate of distinction (red diploma) and a medal. For me, this result confirmed that systematic work, responsibility and genuine interest in learning make it possible to achieve one's goals.

Higher Education

After finishing school, I enrolled at Kuban State University in a programme related to applied mathematics and information technology. My university studies gave me a fundamental grounding in mathematics, programming and computer science, and fostered my interest in the practical application of computational methods.

During my studies, I paid particular attention to programming, data processing and algorithms. Gradually my professional interest concentrated on machine learning, computer vision and work with digital images.

After completing my bachelor's degree, I continued my studies in the master's programme at Kuban State University. One of the directions of my further professional activity became the development of software solutions related to image processing and the recovery of three-dimensional information.

Additional Education and Practical Experience

Seeking to broaden the knowledge gained at university, I completed additional training at Tomsk State University in the field of "Machine Learning". This allowed me to deepen my understanding of data analysis methods, the principles of building machine learning models and the specifics of their practical application.

As part of this additional training, I also completed an internship related to the analysis of a database of computer components. During this work, I gained practical experience in interacting with datasets, studying and analysing them. This experience helped me better understand how information processing methods and software tools can be applied to solve applied problems.

The internship became an important stage in my professional development, as it allowed me to complement my theoretical training with practical data-handling skills and to get closer to the real tasks that arise in the development of information systems.

Professional Skills and Interests

The main programming language I work with is Python. I use it to solve problems of information processing, data analysis and the development of machine learning models. In my work I use libraries and tools from the Python ecosystem designed for data analysis, mathematical computations and building machine learning models.

A special place among my professional interests is occupied by computer graphics and three-dimensional modelling. I work in Blender, creating three-dimensional models of objects and animating them. This experience allows me to combine programming, mathematical methods and the visual representation of results.

I am interested in the field that unites computer vision and three-dimensional graphics. In particular, I am attracted to the tasks of recovering the spatial structure of objects from digital images, building three-dimensional models and automating individual stages of this process.

Research Interests and Plans

The topic of my master's thesis is related to the development of a system for reconstructing 3D models from images. This work examines computer vision methods, algorithms for feature extraction and matching, as well as approaches to building a three-dimensional representation of objects on the basis of photographs.

For me, this topic is of interest not only from a scientific but also from a practical point of view. Three-dimensional reconstruction technologies can be used in computer graphics, virtual and augmented reality, design and other fields where spatial models of real objects need to be obtained.

In the future, I plan to continue developing in the field of machine learning, computer vision and three-dimensional modelling, to improve my skills in developing software systems and to study modern algorithms for processing visual information.

The combination of mathematical training, programming experience, additional education in machine learning and practical work with three-dimensional graphics forms the foundation for my further professional development.
