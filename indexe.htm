Autobiography

Education and School Years

My interest in information technology and modern digital tools gradually developed during my school years. I studied at Gymnasium No. 92 in Krasnodar. During this period, I strived not only to achieve good academic results but also to explore subjects that interested me in greater depth, develop analytical thinking and improve my independent learning skills.

I graduated from the gymnasium with honours, receiving a certificate of distinction and a medal. For me, this achievement demonstrated that consistent effort, responsibility and a genuine interest in learning help to accomplish the goals I set for myself.

Higher Education

After graduating from school, I enrolled at Kuban State University in a programme related to applied mathematics and information technology. My university education provided me with a solid foundation in mathematics, programming and computer science, while also developing my interest in the practical application of computational methods.

During my studies, I paid particular attention to programming, data processing and algorithms. Gradually, my professional interests became focused on machine learning, computer vision and digital image processing.

After completing my bachelor's degree in 2025, I continued my education in the master's programme at Kuban State University. At this stage, I focused on developing my professional competencies and studying software engineering approaches related to image processing and the reconstruction of three-dimensional information.

Additional Education and Practical Experience

To expand the knowledge I had acquired at university, I completed additional training at Tomsk State University in the field of Machine Learning. This experience allowed me to deepen my understanding of data analysis methods, the principles of machine learning model development and their practical applications.

As part of this additional training, I also completed an internship involving the analysis of a database of computer components. During this internship, I gained practical experience in working with datasets, examining their structure and analysing the available information. This helped me better understand how data processing methods and software tools can be applied to practical problems.

The internship became an important stage in my professional development, allowing me to complement my theoretical knowledge with practical data analysis skills and gain experience with tasks encountered in information system development.

Professional Skills and Interests

Python is the main programming language I use in my work. I apply it to information processing, data analysis and machine learning model development. I also work with libraries and tools from the Python ecosystem designed for mathematical computations, data analysis and the development of intelligent models.

Computer graphics and three-dimensional modelling occupy a special place among my professional interests. I work with Blender, where I create three-dimensional object models and develop animations. This experience allows me to combine programming skills, mathematical methods and visual representation.

I am particularly interested in the field that combines computer vision and three-dimensional graphics. I study methods for recovering the spatial structure of objects from digital images, constructing three-dimensional models and automating individual stages of this process.

Research Interests and Future Plans

My master's thesis focuses on the development of a system for reconstructing 3D models from images. As part of this research, I study computer vision methods, feature extraction and matching algorithms, as well as modern approaches to creating three-dimensional representations of objects from photographs.

This research topic is important to me from both scientific and practical perspectives. Three-dimensional reconstruction technologies can be applied in computer graphics, virtual and augmented reality, engineering design and other fields where spatial models of real-world objects are required.

In the future, I plan to continue developing my expertise in machine learning, computer vision and three-dimensional modelling. I also intend to improve my software development skills and explore modern algorithms for visual information processing.

I believe that my background in mathematics, programming experience, additional education in machine learning and practical work with three-dimensional graphics provide a solid foundation for my further professional and academic development.
